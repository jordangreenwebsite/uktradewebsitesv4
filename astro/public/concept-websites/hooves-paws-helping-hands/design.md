# Hooves, Paws & Helping Hands — Concept design
Inspired by Townhouse Clinic's editorial split hero, sophisticated serif typography, generous spacing and restrained motion. Original layout and content adapted for Weymouth pet and practical care.
Brand palette sampled visually from supplied Facebook logo: warm cream #fbf7ec, chocolate #34251d, chestnut #70452b, tan #bd8851 and pale gold #d5b47a.
Services confirmed in Facebook screenshot: long and short dog walks, horse exercising, feeding and ragwort pulling, pet day sitting, older people's shopping, meals and companionship.
