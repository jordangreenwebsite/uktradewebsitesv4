# Hooves, Paws & Helping Hands — Concept design
Inspired by Townhouse Clinic's editorial split hero, sophisticated serif typography, generous spacing and restrained motion. Original layout and content adapted for Weymouth pet and practical care.
Brand palette sampled visually from supplied Facebook logo: warm cream #fbf7ec, chocolate #34251d, chestnut #70452b, tan #bd8851 and pale gold #d5b47a.
Services confirmed in Facebook screenshot: long and short dog walks, horse exercising, feeding and ragwort pulling, pet day sitting, older people's shopping, meals and companionship.
The supplied original logo is displayed as a compact, optimised inline WebP in the header. It remains inside the standalone concept page without changing shared site assets.
Illustrative Unsplash images, not business-owned photos; verify exact photo availability and licensing before official launch.
Desktop: 49/51 editorial hero, serif headings, warm cream, immersive photography, three-column services, dark brown feature and soft area CTA. Mobile: 77px header, accessible expandable menu, stacked hero, full-width CTAs and single-column cards.
Motion: light CSS staggered hero entrance, subtle hover zoom, disabled for reduced-motion. No JS framework.
SEO: one H1, relevant H2s, absolute canonical, descriptive title and meta, semantic HTML, illustrative JSON-LD, noindex/nofollow, no sitemap entry. All links route to https://uktradewebsites.co.uk/ as required for sales concept.
Scope: astro/public/concept-websites/hooves-paws-helping-hands/ only.
