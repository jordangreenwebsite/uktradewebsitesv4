# MM Landscaping | concept design
24 November 2026. Independent sales concept, not the official business website. Inspiration: Townhouse Clinic (https://townhouseclinic.com/) via the archived Ioana adaptation; the original standalone reference is unavailable. Adapt its editorial hierarchy, intentional whitespace and alternating imagery to a strong Pennsylvania trade brand. Crimson #b51832, charcoal #17191b, warm off-white #f5f3ed, silver #c8c8c8. Original logo photographed on the trailer, cropped without redrawing. Bold condensed-feeling sans headings and readable system body type; generous large-screen spacing; full-height lawn hero, thin editorial rules, asymmetric project grid, two-column image comparisons, high-contrast estimate panel and dark footer. Mobile stacks sections, menu toggles with Escape/outside click, native inputs remain keyboard accessible. Motion is limited to short entrance and hover transforms, with reduced-motion fallback. Photos are supplied by the user; prospect approval not independently verified. No invented reviews, awards, guarantees or prices. Calculator values are illustrative USD per visit, not live prices and no lead data is collected. Noindex/noarchive, not in sitemap; personalised UK Trades checkout route via Prunus Media pending maintainer registration. All prospect-facing links go there. No changes outside concept folder.
