Safety comes first

Because a beautiful fire should feel reassuring.

Regular sweeping helps remove deposits that build up during use. How often you need a sweep depends on what you burn and how often you use your fire.

Samson's existing guidance recommends at least twice yearly for wood and coal, and at least once yearly for smokeless fuels, oil and gas.
