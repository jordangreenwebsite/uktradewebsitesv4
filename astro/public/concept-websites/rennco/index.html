ONE TEAM. EVERY DETAIL.

Considered design.
Confident craftsmanship.

Rennco brings together the practical skills needed to reshape your home and garden, from patios and landscaping through to bespoke carpentry and renovation work.

Led by Nathan and Ben, the Newport-based team values straightforward communication, careful planning and a tidy, lasting finish. It's a personal approach to building spaces that feel right from the first sketch to the final detail.
